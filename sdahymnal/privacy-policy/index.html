Privacy Policy for SDA Hymnal

Last updated: July 27, 2026

SDA Hymnal ("the App", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

SDA Hymnal does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or device data. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not require account creation or login
  • Does not access your camera, location, or contacts
  • Does not use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Does not display advertising
  • Does not use crash reporting tools

The App functions entirely on your device without sending information to us or to any third party.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services that collect user or device data.

Data Sharing

Since we do not collect any data, we do not share, sell, or disclose any data to third parties.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, as it does not collect data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
